Questions

Do I need technical skills?

No. You answer a few questions in plain language; Foundrmind writes, designs and publishes. You can copy or download everything it makes.

Is the market research real?

The Niche Profiler searches the live web and links its sources, so you can check every claim. It never invents statistics or testimonials, and it marks estimates as estimates.

Who owns what Foundrmind creates?

You do. Pages, copy, scripts and course material are yours to use, edit and export.

Will you promise me $10K a month?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Foundrmind removes weeks of busywork so you can test ideas faster. Whether they sell is still up to the market, which is why the first module tells you honestly whether an idea is worth pursuing.

Which AI does it use?

Anthropic's Claude, one of the leading frontier models, with live web search for research.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Free stays free. Paid plans will be monthly with no lock-in.
